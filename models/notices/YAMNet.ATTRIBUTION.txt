https://github.com/tensorflow/models/blob/8b12ae202a3ccf8f965c730a4e7617204e32000b/research/audioset/yamnet/yamnet_class_map.csv

https://github.com/audioset/ontology

The YAMNet map selects and reindexes 521 of the 527 AudioSet dataset classes. Recall distributes the published map unchanged. Google's separate CC BY 4.0 license for dataset annotations is not asserted for this ontology-derived map.
